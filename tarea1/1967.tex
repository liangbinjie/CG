        In 1967, György Kepes founded the Center for Advanced Visual Studies (CAVS) at MIT, creating a multidisciplinary community of artists, architects, engineers, and scientists. The center explored how emerging technologies could be used to create large-scale artistic works, with early fellows focusing on areas such as light art and kinetic sculpture.
